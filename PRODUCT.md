# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners, directors, managers, supervisors, and HR or recruitment leads at SMEs in Moldova. They are evaluating whether the HR Toolkit Programme is relevant for hiring, onboarding, and retaining young employees.

## Product Purpose

The landing page explains the donor-supported HR Toolkit Programme, builds trust, and routes eligible companies to express interest through the future external application form.

## Brand Personality

Practical, structured, supportive. The voice should feel credible for business owners without becoming corporate or template-like.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing patterns, stock-card sameness, and designs that hide the programme behind abstract decoration. Earlier rejected directions included an editorial serif version and a more template-like banner direction.

## Design Principles

- Make the programme offer clear in the first screen.
- Use real people, regions, and concrete HR outcomes instead of vague campaign language.
- Keep the Swiss and neo-brutalist structure consistent with the existing cobalt visual system.
- Preserve responsive layouts and multilingual text without clipping or horizontal scroll.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep animation supportive, not blocking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA basics: readable contrast, semantic labels, meaningful alt text, keyboard-friendly controls, and reduced-motion guards for animated elements.
